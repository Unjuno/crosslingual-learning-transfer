# Claims and limitations — frozen scope

**Current authority:** [Final status, 2026-09-10](FINAL_STATUS_2026-09-10.md) and [FINAL_STATUS.json](../results/FINAL_STATUS.json). This file replaces an outdated cross-phase summary, not any locked result or dated report.

## Supported in the documented settings

Controlled A-side interventions can help or harm subsequent B learning. Shared representation/decoder interventions and target-decodable teacher signals affect transfer in the tested synthetic systems. Behavioral dissimilarity can recover some held-out synthetic structural differences, with sensitivity to support changes rather than arbitrary probability-weight changes.

The small domain-limited S2 natural-text trials found English-side favorable directions with target-aware hidden teaching. R1 weight1.0 remains overall FAIL; R2 weight0.5 passes its registered grid-based utility criterion. The margin above five percent is not robust to dense measurement. The fixed-body/common-head diagnostic retains an endpoint body-origin difference, but is exploratory on already-used seeds.

## Explicit non-claims

The record does not establish a universal metric of language distance, a sharp width boundary, a robust selected effective-rank predictor, purely faster learning independent of prepositioned knowledge, semantic competence from byte NLL, target-unaware Japanese-text-only acceleration, independent-corpus generality, human or large-LLM effects, post-English retention, or lower total compute.

A failed specified scalar predictor does not refute every scalar description. Shared-coordinate results in two architectures do not imply architecture-independent effect sizes. A negative fitted slope does not prove strict monotonic improvement at every rank. Descriptive/bootstrap intervals do not repair a failed registered gate. Endpoint-head exchange does not isolate the Transformer block from embeddings and normalization layers.

## Reporting rules

Always name the teacher's target-language information, Student input provenance, learning-history boundary, model/corpus, measurement grid, comparator and evaluation time. Keep a registered verdict and later measurement criticism visible together. Count independent seed cohorts separately from replays; the Sep-9 80-branch diagnostic adds zero independent seeds.
